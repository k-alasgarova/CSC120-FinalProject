THE GOAL
You are an accidental detective on the plane with a murderer. You need to collect 3+ clues  -> identify a killer -> accuse them before the landing.
There are 25 turns, which is intended to make the game more interesting.

ALL COMMANDS
- go [place] - Move to a location
- look - Look around your current location
- talk [name] - Talk to a character
- serve [name] [item] - Give an item to a passenger
- take [item] - Pick up an item
- examine [item] - Look closely at an item
- search - Search the entire crime scene (lavatory only)
- knock - Knock on cockpit door (only cockpit door)
- clues - Review all clues found
- inventory or inv - Check your inventory
- map - See the plane layout
- accuse [name] - Accuse a suspect by having 3+ clues first
- help - Show commands in-game
- quit - Give up

LOCATIONS
- go galley - Galley - The game starts here, with Diane. All supplies are here.
- go economy - Economy Class - Most passengers (and suspects) are here.
- go business - Business Class - Byork (the killer)  and te Diplomat are here
- go lavatory - Lavatories - Crime scene where you use search 
- go cockpit - Cockpit Door - Knock is used here to find clues

CHARACTERS + HOW TO USE THEM (character, location, condition to unlock)
- Diane - Galley - Nothing, she talks straught away
- Mr. Byork - Business - serve byork warm water first
- The Diplomat - Business - serve diplomat meal tray first
- Agnes - Economy - just a character
- Bryson - Economy - just a character
- Lyudmila - Economy - she talks to the player (wanted at first as the player's character but changed to a side character as she adds fun to the narrative)
- Madame Voss - Economy - serve voss pretzel bag first 
- Harold - Economy - just a character
- Dr. Osei - Economy - just a character

ITEMS + WHERE TO FIND THEM (+ how to get them)
- pretzel bag - galley - serve voss pretzel bag
- warm water - galley - serve byork warm water
- meal tray - galley - serve diplomat meal tray
- torn boarding pass - galley - CLUE- take torn boarding pass
- monogrammed cufflink - lavatory - CLUE - go lavatory; search
- folded note - economy - CLUE - take folded note
- empty briefcase - business - CLUE - take empty briefcase

FASTEST PATH TO WIN (an example)
talk Diane
go galley
take warm water
go business
serve byork warm water
talk byork
talk byork (again) <- clue 1
take empty briefcase <- clue 2
go galley
take torn boarding pass <- clue 3
accuse byork <- YOU WIN!!!

THREE ENDINGS (+ how it happens)
1. YOU WIN - accuse Mr. Byork with 3+ clues 
2. WRONG ACCUSATION - accuse the wrong person
3. TIME'S UP - run out of 25 turns without accusing

TIPS
- Some passengers won't talk to you until you serve them something first
- You need at least 3 clues to accuse someone
- Talk to Diane multiple times - she gives a new tip
- Talk to suspects twice - the second conversation always gives the clue
- Every action costs 1 turn - moves, talk, serve, take all count (the clues make it easier to know which one is the killer so 25 turns is enough to add a bit of hardship)
- Lyudmila in seat 14B noticed something! Talk to her!
- Madame Voss is right about everything (she is a psychic, after all), so bring her that pretzel bag!
